Mahjong Blossom Privacy Policy

Effective 30 October 2026. This policy applies to the Mahjong Blossom mobile app (the App) by Happy Apps For You (we, us).

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address or contacts. Your game progress, coins, settings and selected background are stored only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

  • The App does not use your microphone, camera, contacts or precise location.
  • The App may check that your device has an internet connection so that ads and purchases can work.

Advertising

Mahjong Blossom shows banner, interstitial and rewarded ads served by Google AdMob. Rewarded ads are optional and give you an in-game reward when you watch them. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), your IP address and information about how you interact with ads, to deliver and measure ads and, where permitted, to personalise them. Users in the European Economic Area and the UK are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your device settings. See how Google uses data and Google's Privacy Policy.

Premium purchases

Premium features, including removing ads and receiving bonus coins, are sold as a subscription through Google Play. The store processes the payment and tells the App whether your subscription is active. We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status, which may process an anonymous app user ID and purchase information on our behalf. We do not receive your name, email address or payment details. See the Google Privacy Policy and the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with anyone except as described in this policy.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

You can remove all data stored by the App by uninstalling it or clearing its storage in your device settings. Contact us with any privacy request, including under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version.
