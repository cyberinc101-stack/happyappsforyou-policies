Crystal Kingdoms Privacy Policy

Effective 25 October 2026. This policy applies to the Crystal Kingdoms mobile app (the App) by Happy Apps For You (we, us).

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address, location or contacts. Your settings, statistics and any game in progress are stored only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

Advertising

Crystal Kingdoms shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), your IP address and information about how you interact with ads, to deliver and measure ads and, where permitted, to personalise them. Users in the European Economic Area and the UK are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your device settings. See how Google uses data and Google's Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases

Crystal Kingdoms offers optional in-app purchases (such as gems, boosters, a VIP pass and removing ads) sold through Google Play. Google Play processes the payment and we do not receive your name, email address or payment details. The App keeps a record of what you have bought on your device and checks it with Google Play so purchases can be restored. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with anyone except as described in this policy.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

You can remove all data stored by the App by uninstalling it or clearing its storage in your device settings. Contact us with any privacy request, including under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version.
