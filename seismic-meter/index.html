Seismic Meter Privacy Policy

Effective 22 October 2026. This policy applies to the Seismic Meter mobile app (the App) by Happy Apps For You (we, us).

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address or contacts. Your settings, recordings and reports are stored only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

  • Readings from your device's accelerometer are processed on your device. Recordings made in the App are saved on your device only and are not sent to us.
  • Location is optional. If you allow it, your position is read when a recording starts and is saved on your device with that recording so it can appear on the report. It is not sent to us and is never collected in the background. You can deny or withdraw permission in your device settings.
  • When a map is shown, map tiles are requested from OpenStreetMap, which receives the map area being viewed and your IP address. See the OpenStreetMap Privacy Policy.
  • Seismic Meter shows a notification while a recording is running so that Android keeps it active with the screen off and, if you turn on alerts, when your chosen threshold is reached. These notifications are created on your device.

Advertising

Seismic Meter shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), your IP address and information about how you interact with ads, to deliver and measure ads and, where permitted, to personalise them. Users in the European Economic Area and the UK are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your device settings. See how Google uses data and Google's Privacy Policy.

Premium purchases

Premium is a subscription sold through Google Play. Google Play processes the payment. Our subscription provider, RevenueCat, receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase and subscription status so the App can tell whether Premium is active. We do not receive your name, email address or payment details. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with anyone except as described in this policy.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

You can remove all data stored by the App by uninstalling it or clearing its storage in your device settings. Contact us with any privacy request, including under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version.
