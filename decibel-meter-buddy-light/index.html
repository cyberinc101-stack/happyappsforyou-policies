Decibel Meter Buddy Privacy Policy

Effective 22 October 2026. This policy applies to the Decibel Meter Buddy mobile app (the App) by Happy Apps For You (we, us).

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address or contacts. Your settings, measurement history and calibration are stored only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

  • The App uses your device's microphone to measure sound levels. Audio is analysed on your device in real time to calculate decibel readings. The App does not record, store or send audio to us or anyone else. Only the resulting readings (numbers such as the minimum, average and maximum level) are saved in your history on your device.
  • Microphone permission is requested so the meter can work. You can deny or withdraw it in your device settings, but the meter will not work without it.
  • The App may check that your device has an internet connection so that ads and purchases can work.

Advertising

Decibel Meter Buddy shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), your IP address and information about how you interact with ads, to deliver and measure ads and, where permitted, to personalise them. Users in the European Economic Area and the UK are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your device settings. See how Google uses data and Google's Privacy Policy.

Premium purchases

Premium features, including removing ads, are sold through Google Play or the App Store. The store processes the payment and tells the App whether your purchase or subscription is active. We do not receive your name, email address or payment details. See the Google Privacy Policy and Apple Privacy Policy.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with anyone except as described in this policy.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

You can remove all data stored by the App by uninstalling it or clearing its storage in your device settings. Contact us with any privacy request, including under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version.
