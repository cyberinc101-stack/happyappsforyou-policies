4 in a Row Privacy Policy

Effective 23 October 2026. This policy explains how 4 in a Row (the App), published by Happy Apps For You, handles information when you install and use it.

Information we collect

We do not require an account and we do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or microphone data. We do not run our own servers for the App and we do not receive or store any personal information about you.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (levels, stars, coins, gems, unlocked items, settings and statistics) is saved locally on your device only. It is not sent to us. If you uninstall the App or clear its data, this progress is removed.

Advertising

The App shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Depending on your region and settings, ads may be personalised or non-personalised. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings, Google, Ads). See Google's policy at policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses data from apps at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

In-app purchases

Purchases (for example removing ads or buying gems) are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. We only receive confirmation that a purchase was completed so the App can unlock it.

Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with the third-party services described above (Google AdMob and Google Play) as needed for them to operate.

Children

The App is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will help resolve it.

Data retention and deletion

Because we do not collect your data, there is nothing for us to delete. You can remove all locally stored data at any time by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling it. To manage data held by Google, use your Google account's privacy controls.

Security

Your game data stays on your device and is protected by your device's own security. No method of electronic storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
